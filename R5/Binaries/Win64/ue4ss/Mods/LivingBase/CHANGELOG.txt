v2.1.7.1 (2026-08-23) -- previous version: v2.1.7 -- Legacy branch
======================================================================

FIXED -- spawn tree showing the wrong/mismatched entry for several
Walking Women looks
- The bundled spawn_menu.ini still referenced the old pre-2.1.7 roster
  layout (separate Woman With Hat/Woman With Hair entries, old index
  numbers for Merchant/Letty/Marita). Clicking one of those entries
  could spawn a different character than the one labeled. Rebuilt to
  match the current 8-entry roster.

FIXED -- idle (frozen) Senkamati visibly walked around for several
seconds after a world reload before finally freezing in place
- They eventually settled into their correct frozen pose, but the
  freeze call was gated behind the same ~8 second delay used for
  crash-sensitive de-corrupt/passivity post-processing, which it never
  actually needed. Now applied immediately, same frame as the restore
  spawn -- no more visible walking window.

v2.1.7 (2026-08-19) -- previous version: v2.1.5
======================================================================

REBUILT -- Letty, Marita, and the Merchant walking women
- These three now spawn with their own real, distinct outfit instead
  of sharing one generic look with a patched-on overlay. Letty and
  Marita wear their actual in-game outfits; the Merchant wears a
  proper female-fitted Buccaneers Merchant look, fixing a persistent
  pants-clipping issue (a body-shape mismatch baked into her own
  outfit, not a wrong-sex asset -- the earlier v2.1.5 fix for this
  covered the old system only, not this new one).
- Hair and skin tone are still randomized on every spawn, same as
  before.
- Any Letty/Marita/Merchant placed in an earlier version is upgraded
  to her real outfit automatically the next time your save loads --
  no need to despawn and re-place her.

MERGED -- "Woman With Hat" and "Woman With Hair" into one "Woman"
- These two used to be separate entries forcing a hat (or not) onto a
  generic look. They're now a single "Woman" entry (still spawnable as
  either base body) whose outfit, hat presence, hair, and skin tone
  all vary naturally on their own -- no more forced hat logic, and a
  wider range of genuine outfit variety than before.

CHANGED -- Despawn (Num9) and Undo-Despawn (Num0) now work with
In-Game Keys off
- Both now stay reachable with the in-game keybind toggle switched
  off, matching Target Lock (Num+) -- useful for a GUI-driven session
  where the keyboard placement keys are deliberately disabled.

CHANGED -- newly placed items are now the live Target Lock automatically
- Placing anything now locks it as the current live-edit target
  immediately (as if Num+ had just been pressed on it), instead of
  only being available to the diagnostic probe commands. Adjust it
  right away without a separate targeting step.

FIXED -- rotation now respects the live-edit precision setting
- Rotating an object (keyboard ','/'.' or the move window's rotate
  buttons) always used a fixed step regardless of the precision level.
  Now it scales with precision the same way movement already did --
  normal speed rotates exactly like before, finer/coarser precision
  levels now actually apply to rotation too.

CHANGED -- decor/object nudging now moves along a fixed direction
- Sliding a non-NPC object with the live-edit keys used to move it
  relative to whichever way YOU were facing, so the same forward/back/
  left/right press could shift it a different direction depending on
  where you were standing. It now always moves the same fixed
  direction regardless of your facing. Statues and NPCs are unchanged
  -- they still slide along their own pose direction.

CHANGED -- move window slowed to reduce movement-related instability
- The GUI move window's rate of applying nudges is now slower (330ms
  between updates instead of 100ms). Held movement now applies in
  slightly larger, less frequent steps rather than continuous small
  ones -- worth knowing if it feels a little different, but it should
  meaningfully improve stability during heavy live-editing sessions.

FIXED -- "Drops" decor not restoring after a reload
- Placed drop-item decor (Stone/Wood/Leather/etc, and anything else in
  that category) could show up in your save data but come back
  invisible after a reload. The correct visual is now restored
  properly on load. Anything placed before this fix will need to be
  despawned and re-placed once to pick up the correction.

v2.1.5 (2026-08-18) -- previous version: v2.1.0
======================================================================

REORGANIZED GUI SPAWN TREE -- a full hand-curated pass
- The categorized spawn tree (LivingBaseSpawnMenu) has been completely
  reorganized: new top-level sections, new subgrouping, and a real
  friendly name for every entry, replacing the old mechanically
  auto-generated grouping. Covers every roster -- crew, statues,
  townsfolk, walking women, Senkamati, animals, and decor (including
  Drops, positioned alphabetically within Decor).
- Keyboard cycling and lblook/lbspawn are unaffected -- this is purely
  the GUI tree's organization, not a change to what's spawnable or how
  it's reached from the keyboard.

TRIMMED -- crew roster
- Removed 9 crew looks (Smugglers Musketeer/Sailor/Sergeant, Tortuga
  Musketeer/Sailor/Sergeant, Brethren Musketeer/Sailor/Sergeant) as
  part of the tree reorganization. Player Crew, all 3 Buccaneers looks,
  and Brethren Woman remain.

FIXED -- two duplicate Drops labels
- "Reliable Pistol" was used for two different pistol drops -- one is
  now "Worn-Out Pistol". "Clay Tablet" (used for both tablet tiers) is
  now "Clay Tablet 1" / "Clay Tablet 2".

FIXED -- Merchant walking-woman reskin's clipping pants
- Her legs mesh was a male-proportioned piece mismatched to her female
  body, causing visible clipping. Now uses the correct female legs
  mesh from the same armor set.

FIXED -- vanilla Herbalist/Gatherer turning into a random character on reload
- A plain, un-reskinned Herbalist or Gatherer (spawned directly, not
  through the walking-women reskin system) would silently get
  reassigned a random character's look every time the world reloaded.
  Restoring one now correctly leaves it as its own plain look instead.

v2.1.0 (2026-08-18) -- previous version: v2.0.0
======================================================================

FULL 3-AXIS ROTATION -- the GUI's move panel and Coords window
- The move panel's old single-axis Rot L/R + Flip 180 are replaced with
  three full rows -- X, Y, Z (Roll, Pitch, Yaw) -- each with its own
  Left/Right button, so a placed prop can rest at any angle, not just
  spun around its vertical axis. The Coords window's single "Rotation"
  field is now three: Rotation X/Y/Z.
- Keyboard: ',' '.' stay the only two rotate keys -- '/' (the plain key
  next to '.', not the numpad one) cycles which axis they act on, in
  either the game or the GUI window, toast-confirmed each press. Both
  input paths share the same selection, so they can never disagree.
- Persists correctly across reloads and target-lock edits.

DROPS -- a new decor category, browsable in the GUI and by cycling ' in-game
- 18 hand-picked themed categories -- Animal Parts, Artifacts, Clothes,
  Currency, Ingredients, Keys, Meals, Mined, Misc, Potions/Bottles/
  Healing, Seeds, Tailoring, Tools, Treasure, Trophies, Weapons, Wood,
  Writings -- covering everything from weapon and armor pieces to
  currency, ingredients, and treasure.
- Every entry has a real display name (e.g. "Bezoar," not
  "Loot_T02_Bezoar_01") in both the GUI tree and on-screen toasts.

REMOVED -- Blackbeard raid and structure invulnerability
- The Blackbeard raid (drop a flag, trigger a pirate wave, disabled by
  default since v1.3.7) and the PROTECT_STRUCTURES toggle it justified
  are both fully removed from the code now, not just disabled -- lack
  of interest and ongoing maintenance burden.
- The prop that used to be reserved for the raid flag
  (Shared_Camp_PropsComposition_70) is now a normal furniture piece,
  spawnable like any other decoration.

======================================================================

A NEW GUI -- LivingBaseSpawnMenu, a second, compiled companion mod
- The download now bundles LivingBaseSpawnMenu, a real clickable window:
  a categorized spawn tree covering every roster, a held-repeat move/
  edit panel (Forward/Back/Left/Right/Up/Down/rotate as buttons you can

  to the same rosters, not a replacement.

  controls ONLY LivingBase's own keyboard keys; the GUI's buttons are

  now. Starts OFF by default each session (was ON).

UNIQUE PER-PLACEMENT NAMES
- Every placed object now gets its own distinguishable name (e.g.
  "Brethren Woman 1", "Brethren Woman 2") instead of every copy of the
  same look sharing one identical label. Stored in persist.txt, so
  names stay stable across reloads; older saves get names assigned
  automatically the first time each entry restores.

SENKAMATI
- Added a frozen/idle counterpart for the Herbalist-base female Caster
  crew re-skin, matching the already-existing Gatherer-base frozen pair.

v1.3.9 (2026-08-14) -- previous version: v1.3.8
======================================================================

LIVE-EDIT -- target lock + a 2x precision step
- New: Numpad + toggles a target lock. While locked, Num9 despawn, the
  ']'/'[' cycle keys, and every live-edit key (raise/lower/rotate/slide)
  all act on that ONE object instead of re-picking "nearest in front" on
  every press -- so you can back away, turn around, or work at an angle
  without losing your target. Toast-confirms on/off, naming the target;
  auto-releases (with its own toast and reason) if the locked object gets
  despawned, or if you walk more than ~15m away from it (tunable via
  Config.TARGET_LOCK_MAX_DIST). Requires LIVE_EDIT on, same as the rest
  of the live-edit key set.
- The precision cycle (Numpad -) gained a 5th stop: full -> 1/2 -> 1/4 ->
  1/8 -> 2x -> back to full, for fast coarse repositioning.

WALKING WOMEN + SENKAMATI -- a second base body (the Herbalist)
- A pawn's color/palette can't be changed after it's built in this game
  (confirmed a while back) -- so the only real way to get figure/hair-
  color/palette variety is a different underlying NPC class. Added the
  Herbalist (a walking female NPC, same family as the existing Gatherer
  base) as a second option.
- Every Walking Woman look (Numpad .) -- Woman With Hat, Woman With Hair,
  Merchant, Letty, and Marita -- now comes in TWO base bodies, "Base 1"
  (Gatherer) and "Base 2" (Herbalist), for 10 stops total. Same outfit/
  hair-style rules either way (Letty keeps her ponytail on both, etc.);
  only the underlying figure/hair-color/palette differs.
- Senkamati (Num 7): the female Caster's crew re-skin now has a Herbalist-
  base pair alongside the existing Gatherer-base pair (with/without
  helmet each), so the roster grows from 13 to 15 looks.

v1.3.8 (2026-08-13) -- previous version: v1.3.7
======================================================================

PER-WORLD SAVES -- multiple worlds no longer share one base
- Fixed a real bug: Windrose lets you keep several separate named-save
  worlds, but LivingBase was saving everyone's placed crew/decor to ONE
  shared persist.txt/spawn_ledger.txt regardless of which world was
  loaded. Loading a different world restored the WRONG crew into it, and
  saving there overwrote the file the other world depended on.
- Each world now gets its own save file, keyed off that world's own
  internal island ID (read from the game's own world state -- not
  something you need to do anything with; it's automatic).
- Upgrading from an older version: whichever world you load FIRST after
  updating automatically inherits your existing save (nothing lost), and
  that old shared file is then renamed to persist.bak/spawn_ledger.bak so
  no OTHER world can also pick it up. Every world after that starts
  clean, exactly as if it had always had its own save.

CASTER -- improved body fit (used with permission)
- Replaced the previous underwear-mesh patch (used to cover a visible
  pelvis-area gap on the re-skinned human body) with a proper body-mesh
  fix instead, bundled directly with the mod. Removes a seam/gap that
  could show at the hips -- no other behavior changes.

v1.3.7 (2026-08-13) -- previous version: v1.3.5
======================================================================

DECORATIONS -- off the F-row entirely
- Replaced the six individual F1-F6 decoration-category keys with a 2-key
  "active category" system: ';' places one decoration from whichever
  category is currently active, ''' advances which category is active
  (nature -> boats -> wrecks -> tents -> storage -> furniture -> back to
  nature), announced with an on-screen toast/log line so you always know
  what ';' will place next. Changing category doesn't spawn anything by
  itself.
- Motivation: another installed mod (WeatherControl) also binds plain
  F-row keys, and this UE4SS build doesn't reliably distinguish a
  Shift-modified F-key press from a plain one, so two mods bound to the
  same physical F-key both fired on every press. Moving decor placement
  off the F-row entirely avoids this whole class of conflict by
  construction, for any current or future F-row-binding mod, not just
  this one.
- Decor key bindings and category data were pulled into their own file,
  Scripts/fkeys.lua, isolated from config.lua.
- Fixed a real, longstanding restore bug found while validating this:
  decorations were never recognized by the save-restore classifier
  (isStaticLine in spawner.lua), which only checked for posed-statue
  class names. Every decoration has therefore always been silently
  routed into the AI-mover restore path instead of the fast static-actor
  path -- staggered at the slower per-mover interval, and counted in the
  "post-processing N mover(s)..." toast even though decorations never
  needed post-processing at all. Decorations now restore instantly like
  statues, and mover counts/toasts only reflect genuine AI-driven spawns.

BLACKBEARD RAID -- disabled by default
- Config.BBRAID_ENABLED is now false out of the box (was true). The
  feature (raidflag/bbraid keys, the raid trigger itself) is untouched
  in the code and easy to re-enable (Config.BBRAID_ENABLED = true in
  config.lua AND config.txt), but ships off. Removed from the
  R5ModSettings Settings > Mods panel while disabled -- re-add its
  entries in modsettings.lua's M.TOGGLE_DEFS/M.KEYBIND_DEFS if reviving it.

v1.3.5 (2026-08-11) -- previous version: v1.3.0
======================================================================

WALKING WOMEN (Numpad Decimal key)
- New: a walking female NPC that can be re-skinned to look like Letty,
  Marita Suares, or the Buccaneers Merchant -- previously these characters
  could only be placed as frozen, posed statues; there was no way to have
  them wander your base like a normal crew member. Also includes two plain
  looks for general ambient crowd variety, "Woman With Hat" and "Woman With
  Hair". Repeated presses cycle through: Woman With Hat, Woman With Hair,
  Merchant, Letty, Marita, in that order.
- Skin tone and hair now vary too: the two generic looks randomize both on
  every placement, and Letty/Marita/Merchant now randomize skin tone on top
  of their own fixed outfit and hairstyle. Re-rolls fresh on every reload by
  design -- this isn't meant to lock in one permanent appearance.
- Outfit/hair COLOR was investigated and is not possible to change on any
  NPC in this game -- confirmed a build-time-only engine input; forcing it
  earlier crashes. Not pursued further.
- Fixed several real clipping/appearance bugs found through live testing:
  hats not hiding correctly under several different headwear naming
  patterns, a rare case of a forced hat overwriting a torso piece instead
  (toplessness), and "hat hair" persisting with no hat actually placed
  (baldness).
- Placing several of these NPCs and reloading now correctly restores every
  one of them, not just the first of each look -- a queuing bug was
  silently dropping the rest. The "base restored" notification now also
  correctly waits for this restore work to finish before appearing.

SENKAMATI (Numpad 7) roster fixes
- Reloading now correctly restores which of the 14 comparison rows a
  Senkamati actor was (including its helmet on/off state) instead of
  always falling back to one fixed look -- previously undocumented as a
  known gap.
- Fixed a bug where a masked and unmasked Senkamati of the same type,
  placed at the same time, could fight over each other's headdress state
  after a reload, and a separate bug where the saved helmet state wasn't
  being read back correctly at all. Both are now fixed.

======================================================================

SENKAMATI (Numpad 7) -- normal posture for Hunter and Caster
- The Hunter and Caster now walk with a normal human posture instead of their
  native mob's lumbering gait -- the same fix already used for the Warrior,
  applied to the other two for the first time. Under the hood: both are now
  re-skinned onto a walking human-body pawn instead of spawning as the mob
  itself, using the same composite-armor system as the Warrior. The Caster's
  new base is a genuine walking female NPC, found by probing a real townsfolk
  woman already wandering the world (crew pawns are male-only).
- Removed the Healer from the roster -- visually identical to the Caster, not
  worth the extra placements.
- Fixed a real bug found along the way: a de-corrupt rule copied from the
  Caster's mob form was unintentionally HIDING her own Hands/Legs/Feet armor
  the instant it attached, on the new human-body version. Only affected the
  new re-skinned Caster; the original mob form was never affected.
- Fixed a second issue specific to the re-skinned bodies: a visible gap at the
  pelvis where the tribal "fringe" armor piece (designed to show the mob's own
  skin through its gaps) had nothing to show through on a human body. Closed
  with a substitute garment piece for the Hunter and Caster's new re-skinned
  looks. Left off the Warrior at his own request -- barely noticeable under
  his own armor's longer fringe, and he's otherwise spawned exactly as before
  this update (no other changes to his look).
- The Hunter and Warrior can now optionally keep their tribal helmet/headdress
  visible instead of it being hidden by default.
- De-corrupting the Hunter/Caster's ORIGINAL mob-body look (as opposed to the
  new re-skinned one above) is now lighter-touch: it only cleans up skin tone,
  eyes, and hair color. Armor pieces and facial hair that used to be hidden
  are now left visible.
- The re-skinned Caster no longer randomly speaks a townsfolk NPC's idle voice
  lines (a side effect of her new human-body base).
- Num7 now cycles through 14 placements instead of 4, covering every look
  above side by side for comparison: 2 Warrior looks (with/without helmet), 4
  Hunter looks (original mob body vs. re-skinned, each with/without helmet), 4
  Caster looks (same pattern), and 3 "original corrupted" looks (Warrior,
  Hunter, Caster in their native, undiluted corrupted appearance -- still
  peaceful and safe to stand next to, just not visually cleaned up).

v1.2.0 (2026-08-07) -- previous version: v1.1.2
======================================================================

BUG FIXES
- Fixed a bug where StripInteraction and StripQuestScenario silently failed on
  every single spawn: bracket-indexing a UE4SS TArray of components returned an
  unusable wrapper object instead of the component itself, so no interaction
  prompt or quest-scenario component was ever actually being destroyed. Root
  cause found and fixed. Named quest NPCs placed via the mod (Letty, Francois
  Arno, and the new roster entries below) now correctly lose their nameplate,
  interact prompt, and quest dialogue.
- Fixed several diagnostic log messages (spawn failures, composite-look
  resolution) being silently hidden behind the VERBOSE toggle, which made real
  failures impossible to diagnose from the log.

NEW: WALKING FACTION VISITORS
- Crew (Numpad 1) now cycles through 13 looks per press instead of always
  spawning the same default crew pawn: the original look, plus 12 faction-visitor
  re-skins -- Buccaneers, Smugglers, People of Tortuga, and Brethren of the Coast,
  each with a Musketeer, Sailor, and Sergeant look, plus a Brethren of the Coast
  woman.
- These are real crew pawns with their own working AI, so they walk/patrol
  normally -- re-skinned via the game's own composite-mesh system.
- Known limitation: uniform colors are currently generic, not faction-matched.
  An attempt to fix this triggered a fatal native crash on world load and was
  reverted.
- Known limitation: the Brethren of the Coast "woman" look currently presents a
  male body under the female gear. A fix attempt made this worse (broken walk
  animation, still male-presenting) and was reverted.

STANDING STATUES (Numpad 3) -- 15 new entries
- Added 5 confirmed faction leaders / prominent NPCs, all with quest dialogue
  automatically stripped by the bug fix above: Benjamin Hornigold (Brethren of
  the Coast), Henri Boucher (Buccaneers), Marita Suares (Smugglers), and Long Ben
  and Charlie Sharp (People of Tortuga).
- Added 10 more entries found via a full roster audit against the game's own
  asset manifest: DrunkCanoneer, plus missing Merchant and LeanOnWall pose
  variants across all four factions.

SEATED STATUES (Numpad 4) -- 1 new entry
- Added a missing ground-sitter pose variant (People of Tortuga).

INTERACTIVE STATUES (Numpad 6) -- 4 new entries
- Added missing "rummaging a table" and "warming by a fire" pose variants across
  Brethren of the Coast and People of Tortuga.

CHAIR/STOOL STATUES (Numpad 5) -- audited against the game's asset manifest, no
  gaps found.

TOWNSMAN (Numpad 2) -- audited, no gaps found (all 6 Handyman professions were
  already present).

SENKAMATI (Numpad 7) -- 1 new entry
- Added the Healer, joining the Warrior, Hunter, and Caster in the cycle.
- Investigated the Giant Shaker (a boss-tier enemy) but deliberately left it out
  of the roster.

LIVESTOCK / ANIMALS (Numpad 8) -- expanded from 5 to 14 entries

- Added the Sow, Charging Boar, and Boar Mega to the boar family.

- Added the Crocodile and a corrupted "Plague" Crocodile variant.

KEYBINDS

- ']'/'[' collide with the game's own "Change Target" combat bind. The Insert

CLEANUP
- Removed a dead, never-correct guess at "walking faction visitor" crew classes
  that never actually resolved to a real game asset.
- Cleaned a stale leftover keybind entry from the R5ModSettings saved-settings
